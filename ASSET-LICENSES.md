# 3D asset licenses

The two bundled GLB files contain code-generated geometry from [Uchusen Rockets 0.29.0](https://github.com/johnmorrisdotca/uchusen-rockets), used and adapted under the MIT license:

- `assets/starship-mit.glb` — Starship V3 ship geometry, generated from the package's plain-geometry mesh code. The package's painted `starship-v3-ship.glb` is not included.
- `assets/olm-base-mit.glb` — OLM mount and launch tower geometry. Dynamic chopstick arms are generated by Catchy code so they can move during the catch.

Catchy assigns its own materials and colors at load time. The launch-tower width in this build is an independently chosen 17 m gameplay estimate; no OpenStreetMap map or footprint data is included. Model generation uses the upstream MIT code-generated meshes and the GLB output includes no painted third-party assets.

The original MIT license notice is preserved at [`LICENSES/Uchusen-Rockets-MIT.txt`](LICENSES/Uchusen-Rockets-MIT.txt). The upstream package has other separately licensed files; none of its painted CC BY models or ODbL map data is bundled here. See the upstream [NOTICE](https://github.com/johnmorrisdotca/uchusen-rockets/blob/main/NOTICE.md) for its full license breakdown.

The tower's original low-detail tower mesh is hidden at runtime and replaced with a lightweight procedural lattice built for Catchy, with its own materials. Its structural layout and proportions were informed by the MIT-licensed procedural tower in [Starship — Orbital Flight Test](https://github.com/chenhunghan/starship-first-orbital-fight); the applicable notice is preserved at [`LICENSES/Starship-First-Orbital-Flight-MIT.txt`](LICENSES/Starship-First-Orbital-Flight-MIT.txt). The OLM mount mesh remains from Uchusen Rockets.
